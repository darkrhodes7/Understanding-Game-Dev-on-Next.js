## User Questions Answered:

### 1. Rounding Methods Explained:

**FLOOR(Level / 5)** — Rounds DOWN (most conservative)
- Level 1-4: 0 bonus (1d20 flat)
- Level 5-9: +1 (1d20+1)
- Level 10-14: +2 (1d20+2)
- *Best for*: Traditional RPG balance, slow power scaling

**CEILING(Level / 5)** — Rounds UP (more generous)
- Level 1-5: +1 (1d20+1)
- Level 6-10: +2 (1d20+2)
- Level 11-15: +3 (1d20+3)
- *Best for*: Faster progression, keeping low-levels engaged

**ROUND(Level / 5)** — Normal rounding (balanced middle)
- Level 1-2: 0 bonus
- Level 3-7: +1
- Level 8-12: +2
- *Best for*: Smooth curve without extremes

**Recommendation**: CEILING works better for your system because it ensures even Level 1 characters get SOME loot bonus, keeping engagement high.
